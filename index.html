(03) Real builds, not promises

Three recent builds, live right now.

01

Site survey report tool

UK commercial diving · field tool

A technician talks through the visit out loud on their phone. It comes out the other end as a finished PDF report, photos, satellite image, hand sketch and all, no write-up required.

8 fields filled by voice. Zero missed fields.

Built on Next.js and Supabase. OpenAI reads the dictation and the hand sketch, everything forced through structured outputs so the data back is always clean, never a wall of text to tidy up by hand.

02

Finance and inbox automation

education business · back office

Every bank line gets matched to its invoice automatically, coded the way it's always been coded, and dropped into Xero as a draft bill. Nobody has to go hunting for a receipt.

Draft-only. Nothing auto-approved.

Connects directly to Xero's own API and the inbox to find each matching invoice, then codes and drafts the bill. A human still approves everything inside Xero.

03

Content system

marketing · content team

One idea, or the morning's newsletter, goes in. Drafts come out for every channel, written from a personal brief and a company brief, with a cloned-voice video included. Nothing posts until a person approves it.

Approval gate. A reject stops everything.

Claude writes the drafts and video scripts, and ElevenLabs voices the video. Postiz is the single connection to LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, so nothing the AI writes touches a platform directly. The newsletter lands in Beehiiv as a draft for a person to publish.

(07) FAQ

We already build AI systems. Why use you?

For overflow, and for builds outside your usual stack. When you're at capacity, or a client asks for something your team hasn't built, I take that one build at a fixed price, under your name, and hand it back documented.

Will you ever go around me to my client?

No. I don't contact your client unless you ask me to, and a non-solicit clause is part of the standard agreement.

What if the scope changes partway through?

Anything new is a change request, with its own price, agreed before I start on it.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

I offer an upkeep retainer, from £100 a month, for fixes and small changes. Without one, you can still ask and I'll quote it.

What are the payment terms?

50% deposit on our first job together, balance invoiced on delivery, due in 14 days.

What happens to my client's data?

The written scope lists every service the data will touch before I start, usually a database and the AI provider that reads it, and I use only those. A mutual NDA covers everything, and I don't reuse code built for one client on another. Every demo on this page uses test data, never real client data.
